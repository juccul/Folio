Folio Bundled Offline Stroke Recognition Models (folio-stroke-v2)
=================================================================

This directory contains Folio's built-in offline stroke-sequence recognition
models, executed directly on CPU in pure Rust by `folio-recognition`:

  - math_v2.frec    : Math v2 RoPE Conformer Encoder + Decoder (18.58M params,
                      per-channel INT8 symmetric quantization)
  - htr_v2.frec     : Handwriting v2 RoPE Conformer-CTC (16.63M params,
                      per-channel INT8 symmetric quantization)
  - htr_lm.json.gz  : Compact English unigram/bigram + character 4-gram lexicon
                      for CTC prefix beam search
  - pack.json       : Pack manifest (`backend: "folio-stroke-v2"`)

Training Dataset Provenance & Notices
-------------------------------------
1. Math v2 (`math_v2.frec`)
   - Trained on Google Research's MathWriting dataset (CC-BY-4.0).
     https://github.com/google-research/google-research/tree/master/mathwriting

2. Handwriting v2 (`htr_v2.frec`) & Lexicon (`htr_lm.json.gz`)
   - Trained on a combined online stroke corpus consisting of:
     * Penpal handwriting stroke dataset (MIT License)
     * DeepWriting dataset (ETH Zurich — CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; non-commercial research/personal use)
     * BRUSH online handwriting dataset (research use)
     * English word list & n-gram statistics built strictly from training texts
       and standard system English dictionaries.
